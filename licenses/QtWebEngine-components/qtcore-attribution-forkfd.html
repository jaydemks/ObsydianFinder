forkfd

Source: https://doc.qt.io/qt-6/qtcore-attribution-forkfd.html

Used on most Unix platforms in Qt Core.

The sources can be found in qtbase/src/3rdparty/forkfd/forkfd.c, qtbase/src/3rdparty/forkfd/forkfd.h, and qtbase/src/3rdparty/forkfd/forkfd_gcc.h.
